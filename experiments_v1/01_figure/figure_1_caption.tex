\textbf{Left:} The semantic manifold separates into two clusters based on RLHF failure modes.
The \textbf{Alignment Tax Cluster} (Red, 17.6\%) is dominated by strict constraint templates where the open-weights model (Mixtral) outperforms the flagship (GPT-4) by $\Delta = -0.682$ (95\% CI: [-0.738, -0.625]). This inversion occurs because GPT-4's aggressive helpfulness alignment often violates strict formatting negative constraints.
The \textbf{Natural Language Cluster} (Blue, 82.4\%) represents open-ended chat where GPT-4's nuance retains the advantage ($\Delta = +0.133$, 95\% CI: [+0.113, +0.153]).
The difference is highly significant (Mann-Whitney $p < 10^{-140}$, Cohen's $d = 1.90$).
\textbf{Right:} \texttt{banditGPT} identifies this inversion automatically, routing the "Alignment Tax" tasks to the cheaper model not because they are easy, but because the flagship model is \textbf{structurally maladapted} to them.}
