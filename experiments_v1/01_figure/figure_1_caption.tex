\caption{\textbf{Model Preference Heterogeneity in LMSYS Held-Out Prompts ($N=750$).}
\textbf{(A)} PC1 projection (domain-adapted PCA, trained on 80K routing prompts, unsupervised) vs.\ reward gap $(R_{\text{GPT-4-Turbo}} - R_{\text{Mixtral}})$. Reward gaps are discrete pairwise outcomes. The dashed line marks the silhouette-optimal threshold ($\text{PC1}=0.222$), selected without reference to reward labels. The running mean shows a smooth transition in model preference across the PC1 axis.
\textbf{(B)} Outcome proportions by cluster. The Low PC1 cluster (80.8\%, $n=606$, blue) and High PC1 cluster (19.2\%, $n=144$, red) exhibit significantly different outcome distributions ($\chi^2$ test: $p < 0.0001$; Mann-Whitney $p < 0.0001$). The High PC1 cluster shows a substantially higher Mixtral win rate and lower GPT-4-Turbo win rate. Generic C4 PCA (100K web text, no routing connection) confirms a significant but smaller effect ($p < 0.0001$, Cohen's $d = 0.33$ vs.\ $1.53$ with domain-adapted PCA), indicating the domain-adapted PCA amplifies a modest underlying signal.
Reward scores are from LMSYS Chatbot Arena human preference evaluations (discrete pairwise outcomes).}
\label{fig:lmsys_holdout_structure}
