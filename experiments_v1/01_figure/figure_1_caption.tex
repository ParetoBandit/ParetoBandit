    \caption{%
        \textbf{Semantic Task Specialization in Latent Space.}
        We project 80,000 prompt embeddings into a 2D space via PCA, visualizing the density of task difficulty as defined by the measured performance gap ($R_{\text{Strong}} - R_{\text{Weak}}$).
        Blue contours represent regions where Mixtral is sufficient ($|\text{Gap}| \le 0.3$), while red contours denote regions requiring GPT-4 ($\text{Gap} > 0.6$).
        While the two distributions share a central latent region, their centers of mass occupy distinct semantic neighborhoods.
        The overlapping ``Ambiguous Frontier'' highlights tasks where static linguistic features are insufficient for routing, necessitating the contextual online learning provided by BanditGPT.
    }
    \label{fig:semantic_specialization}
