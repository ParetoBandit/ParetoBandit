\caption{\textbf{The Complexity Paradox: Bimodal structure reveals a "Quality Inversion" on hard tasks.} 
We project 1,871 LMSYS prompts onto the first two principal components. 
\textbf{Left:} The semantic manifold splits into two distinct clusters. 
The \textbf{High PC1 Cluster} (Red, 17.6\%) isolates complex coding and reasoning tasks. Crucially, our data reveals a \textbf{Performance Inversion} in this region: the open-weights model (Mixtral) outperforms the flagship (GPT-4-Turbo) by a margin of $\Delta = -0.68$, likely due to reduced refusal and specialized coding priors. 
The \textbf{Low PC1 Cluster} (Blue, 82.4\%) represents general conversational tasks where the flagship model retains a classic advantage ($\Delta = +0.13$). 
\textbf{Right:} This structure flips the traditional routing logic: the "Economic Opportunity" is not just offloading easy tasks, but \textbf{correcting the mis-routing of complex tasks} where the expensive model actively degrades performance.}
