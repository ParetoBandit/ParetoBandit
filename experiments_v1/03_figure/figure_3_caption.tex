    \caption{%
        \textbf{Architecture Diagram: Corralling-Based Router with Constant Exploration.}
        The coordinator manages two LinUCB experts, both using constant exploration ($\alpha = 2.0$) 
        to prevent premature exploitation under domain mismatch: Expert 1 (Warmup) initializes with 
        semantic priors from latent space analysis, while Expert 2 (Tabula Rasa) learns purely from 
        online feedback. Trust allocation adapts via exponential weighting ($\eta=1.0$) with $\gamma$-mixing 
        ($\gamma=0.05$) for expert death prevention. At each request, the coordinator samples an expert 
        with probability $P_t = (1-\gamma)w_t + \gamma/K$, which mixes learned weights $w_t$ with uniform 
        exploration. The selected expert chooses a model, and feedback updates expert weights via 
        importance-weighted loss $\hat{\ell}_t = (1-r_t)/P_t$. We employ constant exploration ($\alpha=2.0$) 
        for both experts, which our ablation studies (§5.4) show outperforms adaptive decay by 48\% 
        (60.6 vs 90.2 regret), confirming that maintaining vigilance is essential under domain mismatch.
    }
