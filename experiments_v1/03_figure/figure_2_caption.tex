        \textbf{Corralled Architecture: Hierarchical Coordination of Warmup and Tabula Rasa Experts.}
        The banditGPT system implements a \textbf{coordinator-expert hierarchy} where a meta-controller dynamically allocates trust between two complementary routing strategies.
        %
        \textbf{(Top)} The \textit{Coordinator Layer} maintains a trust distribution $\pi_t$ over experts.
        \textbf{(Middle)} The \textit{Expert Layer} consists of a \textit{Warmup Expert} (initialized with semantic priors) and a \textit{Tabula Rasa Expert} (learning from scratch).
        \textbf{(Bottom)} The \textit{Feedback Phase} updates the coordinator via importance-weighted losses $\ell = (1-r)/\pi_i$.
        %
        \textit{Note on Values:} The diagram depicts an illustrative snapshot where the Warmup expert retains dominance ($\pi \approx 0.72$), typical of the ``Natural Language'' cluster (Low PC1). However, as shown in the experimental dynamics (Figure~\ref{fig:corralling}), the aggressive learning rate ($\eta=1.0$) allows the system to rapidly invert these weights if the Warmup expert's performance degrades, preventing expert starvation.
