\caption{%
\textbf{Optimal Gamma Calibration Analysis with 32-Component PCA.}
Gamma ($\gamma$) controls how much we trust 80,000 warmup samples vs. 1,121 calibration samples---think of it as a ``volume knob'' for the warmup priors.
We systematically evaluate 8 gamma values to find the sweet spot between leveraging warmup knowledge and adapting to new data.
The router uses corrected RouteLLM data (GPT-4-Turbo wins 68.6\% vs Mixtral's 9.3\%) with 32-component PCA (35.14\% variance).
%
\textbf{(Top-left) Policy Adaptation:} Lines show how GPT-4 usage evolves over calibration samples. Flat lines ($\gamma=1.0$) indicate the router is ``stuck'' at the warmup policy, while steep lines ($\gamma=0.01, 0.05$) show active learning.
%
\textbf{(Top-center) Prior Weakening:} How gamma affects final GPT-4 usage. The baseline (53.3\% at $\gamma=1.0$) shows warmup alone is insufficient. The ``elbow'' around $\gamma=0.01$ (65.7\%) reveals the optimal adaptation point.
%
\textbf{(Top-right) Influence Balance:} Calib/Prior ratio answers ``who's in charge?'' Values near 1.0 indicate balanced influence; $\gamma=0.01$ achieves ratio 1.401 (calibration slightly outweighs warmup).
%
\textbf{(Middle-left) Adaptation Magnitude:} Change from baseline. Zero change ($\gamma=1.0$) means no learning; +12.4 pp ($\gamma=0.01$) shows significant but stable adaptation.
%
\textbf{(Middle-center) Prior Strength:} Effective N shows how many ``equivalent samples'' the warmup contributes. At $\gamma=0.01$, 80k warmup acts like 800 samples---helpful but not overwhelming.
%
\textbf{(Middle-right) Quality:} Average reward on calibration data. Zero reward ($\gamma=1.0$) confirms no adaptation; 0.2284 ($\gamma=0.01$) shows good quality with balanced influence.
%
\textbf{(Bottom) Convergence:} How fast the policy stabilizes. $\gamma=0.05$ converges fastest (0.01346) but sacrifices warmup benefits; $\gamma=0.01$ is slower (0.00125) but more stable.
%
\textbf{Recommendation:} Use $\gamma=0.010$ (highlighted in red) for production. It achieves balanced Calib/Prior influence (ratio 1.401), increases GPT-4 usage by 12.4 pp (53.3\%$\rightarrow$65.7\%), and maintains quality (0.2284 reward). The 32-component PCA captures +6.14\% more semantic variance than 23 components, enabling nuanced routing decisions.
}
