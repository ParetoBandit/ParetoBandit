\begin{figure}[t]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{experiments_v1/03_figure/results/figure3_prior_degradation.pdf}
    \caption{%
        \textbf{Strategy Crossover: When Does Adaptive Safety Pay Off?}
        %
        \textbf{(A)~Strategy Crossover.}
        Cumulative regret vs prior corruption level~$\alpha$ for three
        routing strategies ($N{=}20$ seeds per point, $\pm$1~std bands).
        At $\alpha{=}0$ (correct priors), warmup-only achieves
        30.0~$\pm$~2.0 regret---47\% lower than Corralling
        (44.1~$\pm$~4.7).
        As priors degrade toward adversarial ($\alpha{=}1$), warmup-only
        regret increases sharply to 98+ while Corralling plateaus at
        ${\sim}67$.
        The curves cross near $\alpha{\approx}$\CrossoverWC{}.
        Tabula rasa (flat line at ${\sim}49$) is prior-independent by
        construction and dominates both strategies at high corruption.
        %
        \textbf{(B)~Adaptive Weight Evolution.}
        Corralling's warmup expert weight over 750~queries at three
        prior quality levels (starting from $w_0{=}0.7$).
        With strong priors ($\alpha{=}0$, green), the meta-learner
        correctly increases the warmup weight to ${\sim}90\%$.
        With uninformative priors ($\alpha{=}0.5$, blue), weights
        stabilise near ${\sim}75\%$.
        With adversarial priors ($\alpha{=}1.0$, red), the meta-learner
        reduces the warmup weight toward ${\sim}55\%$, partially
        correcting for prior damage.
        %
        \textbf{Key insight:}
        Corralling's regret range across the full corruption spectrum
        (40--67) is 62\% narrower than warmup-only's (27--99), making
        it the variance-minimising choice under genuine uncertainty.
        Practitioners can validate prior quality on $N{=}100$--$200$
        deployment samples and select the optimal strategy accordingly
        (Table~\ref{tab:strategy_guide}).
        %
        Setup: 750-prompt LMSYS holdout, $\eta{=}\sqrt{\ln 2/750}{\approx}0.030$,
        $\gamma{=}0.05$, $w_0{=}0.7$, $\alpha_{\text{UCB}}{=}2.0$.
        Prior corruption interpolates between correct ($\alpha{=}0$) and
        model-swapped ($\alpha{=}1$) priors.
    }
    \label{fig:prior_degradation}
\end{figure}
