\vspace{0.5em}
\small
\textbf{Validation method:}
Evaluate warmup predictions on $N{=}100$--$200$ deployment samples.
Accuracy $>$80\% = good priors; 50--80\% = uncertain; $<$50\% = bad priors.
Monitor weight evolution: warmup weight $>$0.8 $\Rightarrow$ switch to
warmup-only; $<$0.2 $\Rightarrow$ switch to tabula rasa.

\textbf{Key findings:}
(1)~Meta-learning costs 60\% when priors are strong (48.2 vs 30.0);
(2)~meta-learning correctly identifies the superior expert
    (converges to 70.6\% $\pm$ 11.0\% warmup weight);
(3)~Corralling $\approx$ tabula rasa on this benchmark (48.2 vs 49.2),
    confirming overhead equals prior advantage;
(4)~insurance value manifests under model failure
    (\S\ref{sec:catastrophic}) and zero-shot adoption
    (\S\ref{sec:zero_shot}).
