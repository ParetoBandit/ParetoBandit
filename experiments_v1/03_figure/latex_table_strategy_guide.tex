\textbf{Regret} column: at $\alpha{=}0$ (best case for warmup-only).
\textbf{Range} column: min--max regret across the full corruption
spectrum $\alpha \in [0, 1]$.
Corralling's range (27.2) vs warmup-only's (71.4)
is its distinguishing property under uncertainty.

\textbf{Validation method:}
Evaluate warmup predictions on $N{=}100$--$200$ deployment samples.
Accuracy $>$80\% = good priors; 50--80\% = uncertain; $<$50\% = bad priors.

\textbf{Adaptive workflow:}
Start with Corralling ($w_0{=}0.7$) and monitor weight convergence.
Warmup weight $>$0.8 $\Rightarrow$ switch to warmup-only;
weight $<$0.3 $\Rightarrow$ switch to tabula rasa.
