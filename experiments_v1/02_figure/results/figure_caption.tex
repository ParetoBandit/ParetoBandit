\caption{\textbf{Calibration Convergence Analysis: Policy Shift Occurs During Calibration, Not Holdout.} 
We compare three frozen router policies to isolate convergence effects using \textbf{32-component PCA} (35.14\% variance, +6.14\% vs 23 components) and \textbf{corrected RouteLLM data} (GPT-4-Turbo wins 68.6\% vs Mixtral 9.3\%): (1) Warmup Only uses pre-trained priors from 80K RouteLLM samples with GPT-4-Turbo; (2) $\gamma$-Scaled applies covariance inflation ($\gamma=0.010$, optimal) to weaken priors without calibration data; (3) Fully Calibrated incorporates 1,121 target-domain samples with GPT-4o after $\gamma$-scaling. 
\textbf{(a) Policy Convergence:} Strong model usage drops from 100\% to 16.0\%, an 84.0 percentage point shift representing significant cost savings. Critically, $\gamma$-scaling alone (Scenario 1 $\rightarrow$ 2) produces no change (0.0 pp), while calibration (Scenario 2 $\rightarrow$ 3) drives the full convergence (84.0 pp). This demonstrates that convergence occurs \emph{during calibration}, not during holdout evaluation.
\textbf{(b) Quality-Cost Tradeoff:} The router trades 12.0\% quality reduction (0.971 $\rightarrow$ 0.851 win rate) for substantial cost savings, discovering that the weak model (Mixtral-8x7B) suffices for 84\% of target-domain queries—a rational trade-off for cost-sensitive applications.
\textbf{(c) Bayesian Plasticity:} Effective sample size (Eff. N) quantifies the router's confidence: 324 (highly rigid) $\rightarrow$ 3 (highly uncertain) $\rightarrow$ 35 (moderately confident). The $\gamma$-scaling reduces prior strength by 99.0\%, making the router receptive to new patterns. Calibration data then adds +31 effective samples (9.70$\times$ the weakened prior), sufficient to dominate the posterior and drive the routing strategy shift. This validates our design: covariance inflation creates plasticity; calibration data drives adaptation.
\textbf{Key Finding:} Cross-model transfer (GPT-4-Turbo $\rightarrow$ GPT-4o) succeeds despite capability-tier substitution, demonstrating practical robustness to model evolution. The use of 32 PCA components provides richer semantic representation for more effective calibration.}
