    \caption{\textbf{Zero-Shot Readiness via Heterogeneous Experts and Semantic Transfer.} \textbf{(Left)} Three-way ablation shows Cold Start (red) suffers catastrophic performance drop when GPT-5.1 is released at $t=300$. Warmup Only (orange) improves burn-in but still dips at model release. Warmup + Semantic Transfer (green) maintains peak performance by transferring learned preferences from GPT-4-Turbo ($N=30$ trials, $p=0.0002$ vs. Warmup Only, Cohen's $d=0.77$). \textbf{(Right)} Production router demonstrates statistically significant zero-shot readiness: post-release performance improvement of $+0.62$ reward units ($t_{29}=6.93$, $p<10^{-7}$, Cohen's $d=1.26$). \textbf{Meta-learner dynamics (bottom panel) validate positive transfer}: Conservative expert (blue, initialized with semantic priors) maintains dominant weight ($\sim$75\%) throughout the release event, confirming the transferred preferences correctly identified GPT-5.1 as optimal. The \textit{absence of weight crossing} is evidence of success---if semantic transfer had failed (negative transfer), the meta-learner would have panic-switched to the Adaptive expert (red). Instead, stable weights demonstrate immediate correctness of the prior, eliminating exploration overhead.}
