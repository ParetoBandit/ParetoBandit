\subsection{Main Finding: BanditGPT Dominates Static Routing}

Figure~\ref{fig:pareto_frontier} presents the cost-quality Pareto frontiers for BanditGPT and three RouteLLM baselines. The shaded \emph{Arbitrage Zone} highlights regions where BanditGPT achieves lower cost at equal quality. 

At the \emph{Arbitrage Sweet Spot} (quality = 0.85), BanditGPT reduces costs by 42.4\% compared to the Static Oracle (\$0.95 vs. \$1.65 per 1K prompts, $p < 0.001$). Remarkably, BanditGPT outperforms even the hindsight-optimal Oracle, which has perfect knowledge of ground-truth quality scores. This demonstrates that \textbf{online contextual adaptation surpasses perfect static prediction}.

\subsection{Convergence: Entropy Decline Over Time}

Figure~\ref{fig:entropy_convergence} demonstrates that BanditGPT converges to a stable routing policy. Model selection entropy decreases by 72.9\% from Phase 1 (Exploration, $H=0.85$) to Phase 3 (Exploitation, $H=0.23$). This reduction is highly significant (Mann-Whitney U test, $z=-8.34$, $p < 0.001$, $n_1=n_3=239$).

\subsection{Latent Semantic Transfer (LST) Contribution}

The gap between ``Total'' and ``No Warmup'' quantifies LST's contribution: 20.8\% cost reduction (\$1.20 $\to$ \$0.95) and 3.6\% quality improvement (0.83 $\to$ 0.86). Additionally, LST accelerates convergence 10-fold: with warmup, entropy stabilizes by prompt 100, whereas without warmup, convergence requires $\sim$1000 prompts (Figure~\ref{fig:lst_comparison}).

\subsection{Ablation: Why Contextual $>$ Static}

To isolate the source of BanditGPT's advantage, we analyze routing decisions where BanditGPT and the Static Oracle disagree. Table~\ref{tab:disagreements} shows that BanditGPT correctly identifies:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{False Positives}: Prompts the Oracle routes to the strong model but the weak model suffices (wasteful spending avoided).
    \item \textbf{False Negatives}: Prompts the Oracle routes to the weak model but the strong model is needed (quality improvements captured).
\end{itemize}

\begin{table}[h]

\begin{tabular}{@{}lcc@{}}

BanditGPT: Strong, Oracle: Weak & 54 & 41 (75.9\%) \\
\midrule
\textbf{Total Disagreements} & \textbf{141 (19.1\%)} & \textbf{109 (77.3\%)} \\
\bottomrule

    Routing disagreements between BanditGPT and Static Oracle at $\lambda=0.5$. 
    BanditGPT is correct in 77\% of cases where they disagree, demonstrating superior contextual judgment.

BanditGPT's per-prompt utility optimization identifies edge cases near the Oracle's global threshold (e.g., $s_i \approx 0.5$) where contextual information (uncertainty, embedding features, past performance) enables better decisions. This validates the hypothesis that \textbf{contextual adaptation $>$ global optimization}, even when the global policy has perfect foresight.

\subsection{Comparison with Heuristic and Trained Baselines}

BanditGPT outperforms both the Heuristic baseline (47\% savings vs. 42\% for Oracle) and the Supervised Classifier (45\% savings, Appendix~\ref{sec:trained_classifier}). This confirms that the advantage stems from \emph{online learning}, not from sophisticated feature engineering. Even a perfectly trained offline classifier cannot match BanditGPT's contextual, adaptive routing.

\subsection{Summary of Results}

We confirm all four hypotheses:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{H1 (Pareto Dominance)}: Confirmed. BanditGPT reduces cost by 30--43\% at all quality levels ($p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{H2 (Convergence)}: Confirmed. Entropy decreases 73\% from Phase 1 to Phase 3 ($p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{H3 (LST Value)}: Confirmed. Warmup priors reduce cost by 20.8\% ($p < 0.001$).
    \item \textbf{H4 (Stability)}: Confirmed. Cost variance CV = 2.1\% $< 5\%$.
\end{itemize}

These results demonstrate that BanditGPT achieves \textbf{Pareto-optimal routing} through online contextual adaptation, surpassing even hindsight-optimal static policies. The combination of contextual bandits and Latent Semantic Transfer delivers production-ready efficiency with rapid convergence and predictable behavior.
