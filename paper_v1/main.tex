\subsection{Model Onboarding: Supplementary Analysis}
\label{appendix:model_onboarding}

This section provides supplementary analysis for the model onboarding experiment (Section~\ref{sec:exp4_model_onboarding}).
Figure~\ref{fig:uncertainty_evolution} visualises the uncertainty dynamics that drive Flash's adoption: LinUCB's exploration bonus is proportional to each arm's uncertainty ($\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1})$), so the cold-started arm receives a massive selection advantage until observations shrink its confidence ellipsoid.
The figure also illustrates the forgetting--budget pacer feedback loop for Gemini-Pro: under tight budgets, the expensive arm is rarely selected, geometric forgetting erodes its accumulated information, and uncertainty rises until the inflated UCB bonus triggers an exploratory selection---which briefly reduces uncertainty before the cycle repeats.

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.85\linewidth]{../experiments/04_model_onboarding/results/uncertainty_evolution.pdf}
\caption{Uncertainty evolution during model onboarding (moderate budget, 20 seeds, 95\% bootstrap CI).
  $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1})$ measures total uncertainty per arm: higher values indicate less information and larger exploration bonuses.
  Warm-started arms (Llama-8B, Mistral-Large) maintain low, stable uncertainty (${\sim}0.6$--$2$) throughout both phases.
  Cold-started Flash appears at the Phase~2 boundary with $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1}) = d/\lambda = 26$ (the isotropic prior $A = \lambda I$) and converges to the incumbents' level within ${\sim}300$ steps as LinUCB's exploration bonus drives traffic to it.
  Gemini-Pro exhibits rising, oscillatory uncertainty because it is rarely selected under budget pressure---geometric forgetting erases its accumulated information faster than new observations replenish it, illustrating the forgetting--budget pacer feedback loop described in Section~\ref{sec:methodology}.}
\label{fig:uncertainty_evolution}
\end{figure}
