Budget Cruise Control: \(\lambda_t\)

Route each query to an LLM while keeping average spend per request at target B

Step 1 Smooth the noisy cost signal
$$\bar{c}_t \;=\; (1 - \alpha)\;\bar{c}_{t-1} \;+\; \alpha\; c_t$$

Model costs span 500× — the EMA filters per-request spikes into a stable trend.

Step 2 Update the dual variable
$$\lambda_{t+1} \;=\; \operatorname{clip}\!\Big(\;\lambda_t \;+\; \eta\,\big(\bar{c}_t / B \;-\; 1\big),\;\;\; 0,\;\;\; \bar{\lambda}\;\Big)$$

Positive gradient when overspending, negative when under budget. Clipped to \([0,\,\bar{\lambda}]\).

Overspending  \(\bar{c}_t / B > 1\)
\(\lambda\) rises → expensive models penalized more
→ router shifts to cheaper models → costs drop
vs
Underspending  \(\bar{c}_t / B < 1\)
\(\lambda\) falls → cost penalty relaxed
→ router picks higher-quality models

Self-correcting — like cruise control for your LLM spend
