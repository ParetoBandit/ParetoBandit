  $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1})$ measures total uncertainty per arm: higher values indicate less information and larger exploration bonuses.

  Cold-started Flash appears at the Phase~2 boundary with $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1}) = d/\lambda = 26$ (the isotropic prior $A = \lambda I$) and converges to the incumbents' level within ${\sim}300$ steps as LinUCB's exploration bonus drives traffic to it.
  Gemini-Pro exhibits rising, oscillatory uncertainty because it is rarely selected under budget pressure---geometric forgetting erases its accumulated information faster than new observations replenish it, illustrating the forgetting--budget pacer feedback loop described in Section~\ref{sec:methodology}.}
