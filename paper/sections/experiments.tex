\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experiments}

% TRANSITION from Methodology
We evaluate banditGPT using a held-out LMSYS Chatbot Arena dataset~\cite{zheng2023judging} with fixed train/dev/test splits, and commercial per-request costs for each model. Additional evaluations (multi-model scaling, alternative bandit baselines, regret curves, and robustness stress tests) are reported in the appendix (supplementary material).

\subsection{Dataset \& Ground Truth}
We use three disjoint prompt sets to avoid leakage: (i)~offline data for warmup priors (80k RouteLLM battles for the Mixtral/GPT-4-Turbo pair), (ii)~a dev stream of 1,121 prompts for online bandit updates, and (iii)~a frozen holdout of 750 prompts for evaluation of cost--quality trade-offs. Full dataset provenance and split details are provided in the appendix (supplementary material).

\subsection{Model Topology \& Baselines}

We mirror RouteLLM's primary $K{=}2$ evaluation topology~\cite{ong2024routellm}:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Weak Model}: \texttt{mistralai/mixtral-8x7b-instruct} (Cost: $\approx$ \$0.30 / 1M tokens).
    \item \textbf{Strong Model}: \texttt{openai/gpt-4-turbo} (Cost: $\approx$ \$10.00 -- \$30.00 / 1M tokens).
\end{itemize}

We compare against static single-model baselines, random routing, RouteLLM-MF~\cite{ong2024routellm}, and an oracle upper bound that selects the better model per prompt.

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}

We evaluate performance using three key metrics standard in cost-aware routing literature:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Average Reward ($\bar{r}$)}: The mean quality score of the selected responses.
    
    \item \textbf{Normalized Cost ($\bar{c}$)}: The average inference cost, normalized such that GPT-4-Turbo $\approx 1.0$.
    
    \item \textbf{Gap Closure (\%)}: The percentage of the performance gap between the Weak Model and the Oracle that the router recovers, defined as:
    \begin{equation}
    \text{Gap Closure} = \frac{R_{\text{router}} - R_{\text{weak}}}{R_{\text{oracle}} - R_{\text{weak}}} \times 100\%
    \end{equation}
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Implementation Details}

\textbf{Embeddings:} \texttt{all-MiniLM-L6-v2}~\cite{reimers2019sentence} with 32D PCA trained on 80k routing prompts.
\textbf{Trials:} Unless noted otherwise, we report mean and 95\% confidence intervals over 20 seeds for each cost penalty $\lambda$ in the sweep. Additional statistical details and robustness checks are in the appendix (supplementary material).
