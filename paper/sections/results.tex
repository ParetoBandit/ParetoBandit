\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:results}

We evaluate \textit{banditGPT} through the following research questions:

\begin{itemize}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{RQ1}: How does banditGPT's cost--quality Pareto frontier compare to supervised routing baselines?
    \item \textbf{RQ2}: Does Corralling provide resilience against catastrophic model failure?
    \item \textbf{RQ3}: How robust is banditGPT to distribution shift and prior mismatch?
    \item \textbf{RQ4}: How does banditGPT compare to alternative bandit baselines, and how quickly does it converge?
\end{itemize}

\subsection{RQ1: Cost--Quality Trade-off}
\label{sec:pareto}

We compare \textit{banditGPT} against supervised routing baselines from LLMRouter~\cite{ding2024hybrid} (KNN, SVM, MLP) and standard online baselines.
All routers are trained on the same 533-prompt online-learning split; supervised baselines receive the full per-model reward matrix while \textit{banditGPT} observes only the reward of the model it selects (partial feedback).
Performance is evaluated on the frozen 750-prompt holdout using Pareto AUC, PerfGain, CostSave, and Gap@Oracle~\cite{hu2024routerbench}.

\subsection{Additional Evaluations and Limitations}
\label{sec:robustness_adaptation}
\label{sec:prior_degradation}
\label{sec:transfer_null}
\label{sec:limitations}

Additional analyses in the supplementary material include robustness under prior mismatch, synthetic catastrophic-failure stress tests, and a dynamic model onboarding demonstration (Appendix~\ref{app:model_onboarding_figure}) showing that \texttt{register\_model()} seamlessly incorporates a new model into the portfolio via UCB exploration.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Different portfolios and traffic will shift quantitative crossover points. While our distribution shift analysis (Section~\ref{sec:distribution_shift}) shows adaptation renders prior source statistically irrelevant, magnitudes will vary with distributional mismatch. Our current treatment of non-stationarity relies on meta-level mixing rather than explicit forgetting within experts.
