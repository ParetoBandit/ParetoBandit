\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

As model portfolios diversify, LLM routing becomes less about a fixed ``cheap vs.\ strong'' ordering and more about identifying which prompts benefit from which models under a budget. We presented \textbf{banditGPT}, an open-source contextual bandit framework for cost-aware routing with online adaptation and a meta-learning hedge against imperfect priors.

Our evaluation on $N{=}750$ held-out LMSYS Arena prompts establishes three findings:
\begin{enumerate}[nosep]
    \item \textbf{Online adaptation can improve routing at moderate-to-high budgets.} In our two-model benchmark, banditGPT surpasses RouteLLM's peak quality after $\sim$300--400 label-free in-distribution prompts and achieves 70.0\% gap closure to oracle (vs.\ 46.2\% for RouteLLM). At low budgets, RouteLLM remains competitive, highlighting a practical trade-off between offline supervision and online adaptation.
    \item \textbf{Corralling helps when priors are useful, and consistently improves over warmup-only.} The meta-learner combines complementary exploration schedules and reduces regret by 7--13\% relative to warmup-only across all corruption levels ($N{=}20$ seeds). At low corruption ($\alpha \leq 0.2$), Corralling also modestly outperforms tabula rasa (${\sim}6\%$ lower regret). Under strongly misleading priors ($\alpha \geq 0.3$), Corralling's advantage over tabula rasa disappears; we show this regime is detectable in our stress tests and can be handled with a simple tabula rasa fallback (Section~\ref{sec:prior_degradation}).
    \item \textbf{Continuous family sharing subsumes one-shot transfer.} A leave-one-out ablation confirms that per-model $\thetavec$-transfer provides no significant benefit beyond the continuous family $\betavec_F$ sharing of Hybrid LinUCB ($p{>}0.5$, $d{<}0.05$ at $K{=}10$). In our evaluation, this suggests that the hybrid decomposition is an effective abstraction level for cross-model knowledge transfer: practitioners can benefit from data-driven family grouping without investing in per-model bootstrapping pipelines.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Future Work}
Key open problems include expert-level non-stationarity handling, adaptive exploration schedules that reduce the quality tax in stationary regimes, reward-aware family assignment for cross-provider model groups, and extending banditGPT's Corralling mechanism from single-model routing to agentic orchestration of autonomous agent portfolios. Detailed future directions are provided in Appendix~\ref{sec:future_work_detailed}.
