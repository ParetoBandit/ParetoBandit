\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

As model portfolios diversify, LLM routing evolves from a fixed static ordering into discovering which specific prompts benefit from which models under a budget constraint. We presented \textbf{banditGPT}, an open-source contextual bandit framework that combines online adaptation with a meta-learning hedge against imperfect priors. Our evaluation establishes three core findings:

\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Online adaptation excels at scale:} banditGPT's 95\% CI lower bound surpasses state-of-the-art static routers after ${\sim}400$ in-distribution prompts, achieving 61.5\% gap closure to the theoretical oracle for $K{=}2$ and Pareto-dominating baselines across cost spectrums at up to $K{=}10$.
    \item \textbf{Corralling provides safety:} The meta-learner consistently reduces regret relative to single-expert bandits, providing a safety mechanism when offline priors fail or drift occurs.
    \item \textbf{Family sharing enhances stability:} A data-driven approach to Hybrid LinUCB sharing shrinks confidence intervals by ${\sim}5\times$ at $K{=}5$ ($p{=}0.028$) when per-model data is scarce, though the advantage attenuates at larger portfolios as per-model observations suffice for independent convergence.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Deployment applicability.}
These mechanisms compose to address diverse production requirements.  A cost-constrained SaaS startup can enforce hard per-request budget ceilings via \texttt{max\_cost} while a soft cost penalty $\lambda{=}0.7$ steers remaining traffic to the cheapest successful model (Appendix~\ref{app:scenario_cost}).  A latency-sensitive IDE plugin can filter models by TTFT and rely on Corralling to recover when traffic distributions shift (Appendix~\ref{app:scenario_latency}).  A quality-critical legal-tech deployment can set $\lambda{=}0.0$ for pure quality optimisation and onboard a fine-tuned specialist mid-stream via \texttt{register\_model()} with zero downtime (Appendix~\ref{app:scenario_quality}).  Worked examples with reproducible code are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:deployment_scenarios}.

\paragraph{Future Work.} Future trajectories include adopting neural contextual bandits~\cite{atalar2025neural} to capture non-linear reward mappings and extending our hierarchical Corralling meta-learner to \emph{agentic orchestration}~\cite{wu2023autogen}, moving beyond model routing to selecting multi-agent strategies dynamically.
