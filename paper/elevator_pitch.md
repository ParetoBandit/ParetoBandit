# The "Elevator Pitch" for KDD

"Existing bandit routers suffer from a 'Trilemma': they either require expensive full-information training (Li), rely on sparse human data (Panda), or suffer from cold-start instability (Dai, BaRP). BanditGPT resolves this by introducing Procedural Warmup: we use cheap synthetic data to pre-compute the feature geometry, allowing a linear bandit to deploy with 'Senior Engineer Intuition' from Day 1, at zero marginal cost."
