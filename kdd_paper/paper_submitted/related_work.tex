\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Existing LLM routing approaches fall into three categories, each with critical limitations that BanditGPT addresses.

\textbf{Cascading systems} like FrugalGPT~\cite{chen2023frugalgpt} and RouteLLM~\cite{ong2024routellm} achieve cost savings through sequential model calls, but suffer from $O(N)$ latency scaling and fixed 2--3 model chains that miss specialist opportunities. More critically, they rely on \emph{ex-post verification} that fails on ``Confident Failures''---plausible-sounding wrong answers that fool verifiers (86\% vs our 96\% on instructions). Our $O(1)$ vector search over 80+ models, combined with \emph{ex-ante prediction}, eliminates both bottlenecks.

\textbf{Contextual bandits}~\cite{li2010contextual} offer adaptive routing but require $\sim$2,000 queries (\$50--200 exploration cost) before convergence---prohibitive for production deployment. Our \textbf{Expert Distillation} bootstraps from teacher priors, achieving 63.6\% regret reduction from day one without exploration overhead.

\textbf{Static leaderboards} (LMSYS~\cite{zheng2023judging}, HELM~\cite{liang2022holistic}) provide global rankings but ignore cost and average across all users. We build a personalized, cost-aware ranking optimized for each deployment's utility function.
