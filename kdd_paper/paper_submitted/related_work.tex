\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

We address: \emph{``Why not use existing solutions?''}

\subsection{LLM Routing and Cascading}

\textbf{FrugalGPT}~\cite{chen2023frugalgpt} and \textbf{RouteLLM}~\cite{ong2024routellm} demonstrate cost-saving potential but have production limitations:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{$O(N)$ Latency}: Cascading requires sequential calls, doubling latency for hard queries. We route in \textbf{<9ms}.
    
    \item \textbf{Fixed Chains}: FrugalGPT is limited to 2--3 models. With 80+ specialists, you miss cheap domain experts (e.g., BioMistral for CRISPR queries). Our $O(1)$ vector search unlocks the long tail.
    
    \item \textbf{Verification Failure}: FrugalGPT relies on \emph{ex-post verification}, but cheap models produce plausible-sounding wrong answers that fool verifiers (``Confident Failures''). We use \emph{ex-ante prediction}: 96\% vs 86\% on instructions.
    
    \item \textbf{Drift Intolerance}: Supervised routers require retraining monthly. Our bandit adapts online.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contextual Bandits}

Standard bandits~\cite{li2010contextual} suffer from \textbf{Cold-Start}: 81 models $\times$ 384 dims needs $\sim$2,000 queries (\$50--200 exploration cost, weeks to converge). We solve this via \textbf{Expert Distillation}---bootstrapping from teacher priors for 63.6\% regret reduction from day one.

\subsection{Static Leaderboards}

LMSYS~\cite{zheng2023judging}, HELM~\cite{liang2022holistic} provide global rankings but (1) ignore cost, and (2) average across all users. We build a \textbf{personalized leaderboard} optimizing \emph{your} utility function.
