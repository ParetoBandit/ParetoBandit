\section{Cost-Quality Efficiency}
\label{sec:rq3}

Can intelligent routing simultaneously improve quality \emph{and} reduce cost? We demonstrate that BanditGPT breaks the traditional cost-quality trade-off, achieving both higher accuracy and lower cost than static baselines.

\paragraph{The Efficiency Frontier.} Figure~\ref{fig:pareto} visualizes the cost-accuracy landscape. BanditGPT occupies two points on the Pareto frontier: \textbf{Standard Mode} (cost leader at \$0.67/1k) and \textbf{Hybrid Mode} (high-assurance, approaching FrugalGPT accuracy with $O(1)$ scalability). The cascade rate $\lambda$ allows users to smoothly interpolate between these operating points.

    \centering

    \caption{\textbf{Pareto Frontier.} Left: Cost vs.\ System Accuracy. BanditGPT Standard achieves lowest cost; Hybrid approaches FrugalGPT with $O(1)$ scalability. Right: Domain breakdown shows Hybrid beats FrugalGPT on Instructions (98\% vs 95\%).}
    \label{fig:pareto}
\end{figure}

\paragraph{Breaking the Trade-off.} Table~\ref{tab:system_impact} quantifies the improvement over static policies. The adaptive router \emph{beats} GPT-4o on quality (0.67 vs 0.63) while costing 67.7\% less. This is not a compromise---it's a strict improvement on both axes. The router achieves this by using GPT-4o only when necessary (31\% of queries), routing the majority to 97\% cheaper specialists like Nova-Lite (39.4\%) and Nova-Micro (29.6\%).

\paragraph{The Business Case.} Why does this work? Top specialists deliver \textbf{90×+ better ROI} than GPT-4o (Table~\ref{tab:roi}). Nova-Micro provides 93× more learned expertise per dollar---for queries where it succeeds, using GPT-4o wastes 93\% of budget. At production scale (1M queries/month), GPT-4o costs \$2,190 while our Adaptive Router costs \textbf{\$710}---annual savings of \$17,760 with \emph{improved} quality. At 10M queries/month: \textbf{\$178,000 annual savings}.
