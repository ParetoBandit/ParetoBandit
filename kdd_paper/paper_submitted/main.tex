% ABSTRACT
% ============================================================================
\begin{abstract}
The expansion of the Large Language Model (LLM) ecosystem has created a ``Long Tail'' of specialized models that offer high performance at a fraction of the cost of frontier generalists. However, existing routing systems fail to efficiently capitalize on this fragmentation: cascading frameworks (e.g., FrugalGPT) suffer from linear latency scaling ($O(N)$) and rely on rigid, small-chain configurations that overlook niche specialists.

In this work, we introduce \textbf{BanditGPT}, a scalable routing framework that utilizes ``Shippable Priors''---covariance matrices calibrated offline---to enable $O(1)$ predictive search over massive model pools. Unlike naive cold-start bandits that require thousands of interactions to converge, our approach leverages \textbf{Few-Shot Domain Calibration} (labeling $<$100 examples) to transfer learned model correlations to new domains, reducing Day 1 regret by \textbf{96--99\%} on held-out benchmarks.

We propose a \textbf{tiered architecture} that combines prediction and verification:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Standard Mode (Cost-Optimal)}: A single-shot predictive router that defines a new Pareto frontier, achieving 92.0\% accuracy at \$0.46/1k queries---a \textbf{71\% cost reduction} compared to FrugalGPT (\$1.60) and 63\% compared to DeepSeek V3, while maintaining equal accuracy.
    
    \item \textbf{Hybrid Mode (Bandit-Guided Cascade)}: A high-assurance integration where the bandit acts as a ``Gatekeeper'' to a verification cascade. This addresses the ``Confident Failure'' phenomenon in instruction-following tasks, using \emph{ex-ante} prediction to bypass weaker models on complex constraints.
\end{itemize}

Finally, we introduce a linearly calibrated \textbf{Cascade Rate} parameter ($\lambda$) that allows users to dynamically navigate the Pareto frontier. By strictly controlling the percentage of queries routed to the expensive verifier (from 0\% to 100\%), users can precisely align inference costs with their financial SLAs. Our results establish BanditGPT as a robust solution for both cost-constrained and quality-critical environments.
\end{abstract}
