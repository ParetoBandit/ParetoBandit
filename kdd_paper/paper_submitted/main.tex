% ABSTRACT
% ============================================================================
\begin{abstract}
Large Language Model (LLM) routing---selecting the optimal model for each query from a heterogeneous pool---offers significant cost savings but faces a critical cold-start problem: online learning methods require hundreds of costly exploration queries before becoming effective. We introduce a \textbf{Density-Based Warm-Start Framework} that solves this through \emph{Expert Distillation}: compressing the latent expertise of 81 models into a lightweight (<1MB) covariance prior that ships with the library. Our ``Shippable Brain'' approach provides day-one intelligence while retaining full plasticity for local adaptation. 

Evaluated on 2,000 real-world queries, our framework achieves: (1) \textbf{63.6\% regret reduction} vs.\ cold-start baselines, (2) \textbf{97.6\% cost savings} on equal-quality queries, and (3) \textbf{+6.1\% higher quality} than static GPT-4o routing at 67.7\% lower cost. Router overhead is just 8.94ms (P99), representing 1.1\% of total latency. We release BanditGPT, an open-source implementation with bundled expert priors.
\end{abstract}
