% ABSTRACT
% ============================================================================
\begin{abstract}
The exploding LLM ecosystem has created a ``Long Tail'' of specialized models offering high performance at a fraction of frontier costs. However, existing routing systems fail to capitalize on this fragmentation: cascading frameworks (e.g., FrugalGPT) suffer from $O(N)$ latency and rigid chains that miss niche specialists.

We introduce \textbf{BanditGPT}, a scalable routing framework using ``Shippable Priors''---covariance matrices calibrated offline---to enable $O(1)$ predictive search over massive model pools. Unlike cold-start bandits requiring thousands of interactions, our \textbf{Few-Shot Calibration} ($<$100 examples) transfers learned correlations to new domains, reducing Day 1 regret by \textbf{96--99\%}.

We propose a \textbf{tiered architecture}: (1) \textbf{Standard Mode} achieves 92.0\% accuracy at \$0.46/1k queries---\textbf{71\% cheaper} than FrugalGPT; (2) \textbf{Hybrid Mode} uses the bandit as a ``Gatekeeper'' to a verification cascade, addressing the ``Confident Failure'' phenomenon where verifiers approve plausible-but-wrong outputs, outperforming FrugalGPT by \textbf{+3\%} on instruction tasks (\textbf{98.0\% vs 95.0\%}) via \emph{ex-ante} prediction. A tunable \textbf{Cascade Rate} ($\lambda$) lets users navigate the Pareto frontier to align costs with financial SLAs.
\end{abstract}
