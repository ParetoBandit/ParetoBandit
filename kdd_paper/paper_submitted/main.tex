% ABSTRACT
% ============================================================================
\begin{abstract}
Large Language Model (LLM) routing is typically framed as a choice between a cheap ``Weak'' model and an expensive ``Strong'' model. However, the open model ecosystem has expanded to hundreds of specialized variants. Existing methods like Cascades (FrugalGPT) struggle to scale beyond 2--3 models due to $O(N)$ latency penalties, and rely on \emph{ex-post verification} that fails on complex tasks.

We introduce \textbf{BanditGPT}, a scalable routing framework combining ``Shippable Priors''---offline-calibrated covariance matrices---with \emph{ex-ante prediction} to enable $O(1)$ search over 81+ models. Our key insight: \textbf{prediction beats verification} when ``checking the work'' is as hard as ``doing the work.'' On instruction-following tasks, FrugalGPT's cascade falls victim to ``Confident Failures''---plausible outputs that fool verifiers---achieving only 86\% accuracy. BanditGPT detects these high-risk prompts \emph{before} generation and routes directly to GPT-4o, achieving \textbf{96\% accuracy (+10\%)}.

Our results establish BanditGPT on the \textbf{Pareto Frontier}: 92.8\% aggregate accuracy at \$1.24/1k queries (\textbf{24\% cheaper} than FrugalGPT at \$1.63/1k). While Cascades achieve marginally higher aggregate accuracy (95.2\%), they critically fail on complex constraint tasks. BanditGPT provides \textbf{day-one intelligence} via expert-distilled priors (63.6\% regret reduction vs.\ cold-start), making it the state-of-the-art solution for cost-constrained, high-reliability environments.
\end{abstract}
