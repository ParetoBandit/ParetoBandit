\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{BanditGPT}, a context-aware routing system that eliminates the cold-start problem via \textbf{Expert Distillation}---bootstrapping from a teacher oracle's covariance structure compressed to <1MB.

\paragraph{Key Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Cold-Start Elimination}: Expert priors achieve \textbf{63.6\% regret reduction} from day one. Few-Shot Calibration ($\sim$50--100 examples) enables \textbf{93.5\% regret reduction} on held-out benchmarks.
    
    \item \textbf{Pareto Efficiency}: BanditGPT achieves +6.1\% quality and $-$67.7\% cost vs.\ GPT-4o. On instruction tasks, Hybrid mode beats FrugalGPT (98\% vs 95\%) by predicting complexity \emph{before} generation.
    
    \item \textbf{Production Ready}: 8.94ms P99 routing overhead (1.1\%), self-correcting plasticity (recovers from bad priors in $\sim$200 steps), and single-pass routing over 81+ models.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{The Paradigm Shift.} Specialized models---when orchestrated by an expert-guided router---collectively outperform the frontier models they were distilled from. Nova-Lite at \$0.10/1M tokens, guided by our router, delivers higher quality than GPT-4o at \$4.38/1M tokens.
