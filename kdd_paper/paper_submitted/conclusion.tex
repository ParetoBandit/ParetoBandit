\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we introduced the \textbf{Shippable Router}, a context-aware bandit system that operationalizes the ``Mixture of Agents'' paradigm for real-time production environments. By replacing standard random initialization with \textbf{Expert Distillation}---bootstrapping from a teacher oracle's covariance structure---we successfully eliminated the ``Cold-Start'' penalty that has historically hindered the adoption of online learning in LLM orchestration.

Our empirical evaluation on real-world routing traces demonstrates three critical contributions:

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Beat 1: Technical Victory (Cold Start Solved)
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{1. Cold-Start Elimination.}
Expert-distilled priors, compressed to <1MB, provide \textbf{day-one intelligence} without exploration cost. RQ1 demonstrated \textbf{63.6\% regret reduction} compared to cold-start baselines---the gap represents real dollars saved from the first query. This ``Shippable Brain'' paradigm transforms LLM routing from a months-long calibration process into an instant deployment.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Beat 2: Business Victory (Cost/Quality Trade-off Broken)
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{2. Economic Efficiency.}
The router identifies a non-linear Pareto frontier, achieving a \textbf{67.7\% reduction} in aggregate inference costs while \emph{simultaneously improving} response quality by \textbf{6.1\%} compared to a static GPT-4o baseline. On 45.5\% of queries where specialist and generalist models perform equally, the router captures \textbf{97.6\% cost savings} with zero quality degradation. This is not a trade-off---it is a free lunch enabled by intelligent specialist discovery.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Beat 3: System Victory (Production-Ready)
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{3. Plasticity and Production Readiness.}
Unlike static benchmark-based routers, our system exhibits high \textbf{plasticity}---successfully recovering from ``Confidently Wrong'' priors in under 200 interactions (RQ2)---while maintaining \textbf{8.94ms latency} (P99), representing just 1.1\% of total request time. The system is not merely theoretically sound; it is engineered for latency-critical production environments.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% Forward Compatibility
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{Forward Compatibility.}
By decoupling routing logic from static leaderboards, we enable a sustainable deployment lifecycle. New frontier models can be instantly added as ``cold arms'' and evaluated dynamically via the bandit's exploration mechanism. The router adapts to capability drift, API changes, and emerging specialists---no manual retraining required.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
% The Paradigm Shift
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

\paragraph{The Paradigm Shift.}
Ultimately, this work \textbf{democratizes access to state-of-the-art intelligence}. We prove that specialized, cost-effective models---when orchestrated by a confident, expert-guided router---can collectively \emph{outperform} the very teacher models they were distilled from. Nova-Lite at \$0.10/1M tokens, guided by our router, delivers higher aggregate quality than GPT-4o at \$4.38/1M tokens.

This is the future of LLM deployment: not a single ``best'' model, but an intelligent ensemble that routes each query to its optimal specialist. The Shippable Router makes this future accessible today---with zero cold-start cost, sub-10ms latency, and open-source availability.
