\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this work, we presented \ours{}, a framework that transitions LLM routing from static, reactive cascades to scalable, predictive decision-making. By distilling offline teacher supervision into ``Shippable Priors,'' we resolve the cold-start problem that has historically prevented the adoption of contextual bandits in production, achieving a \textbf{64.6\% reduction in Day-1 regret} and enabling effective routing from the very first query.

\paragraph{Business-Adaptive Routing via Tunable Objectives.}
Crucially, our architecture recognizes that ``optimality'' is not a universal constant but a business-specific constraint. Through the reward function's sensitivity parameters ($\lambdacost, \lambdalat$), \ours{} offers organizations precise control over their operational trade-offs. A FinOps-focused team can aggressively penalize cost ($\lambdacost \gg 0$) to enforce budget caps, while a user-facing application can penalize latency ($\lambdalat \gg 0$) to guarantee SLAs. This transforms routing from a rigid technical hurdle into a flexible lever for business alignment.

\paragraph{Future-Proofing Beyond Benchmarks.}
Finally, \ours{} addresses the scalability crisis in the rapidly expanding model ecosystem. Traditional routers relying on static benchmarks face a ``Calibration Bottleneck'' ($O(N)$), requiring expensive offline profiling for every new model release. Furthermore, as public benchmarks saturate and diverge from real-world utility, static rankings become increasingly unreliable.

Our framework decouples \emph{Prompt Difficulty} (learned once) from \emph{Model Capability} (verified online). By using public metadata merely as coarse initialization filters, \ours{} shifts the burden of proof from offline benchmarks to online exploration. This allows the system to onboard new models in $O(1)$ time---simply registering an API endpoint---and autonomously discover their utility on live traffic. As the ``Long Tail'' of specialized models grows, \ours{} ensures that production systems can instantly leverage these efficient specialists without the maintenance burden of fixed chains.
