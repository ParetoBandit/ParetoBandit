\section{Qualitative Analysis}
\label{sec:qualitative}

A natural concern: \emph{``Are routing decisions semantically meaningful?''} We present representative cases.

\paragraph{Case 1: Specialist Win.} \emph{``Generate a SQL query to find top 5 customers by order value.''} $\to$ Nova-Lite (1.00) over GPT-4o (0.00). SQL is rigid syntax where smaller models excel.

\paragraph{Case 2: Teacher Fallback.} \emph{``What is the definite integral of $\sin(x)/x$ from 0 to $\infty$?''} $\to$ GPT-4o (1.00) over Nova-Lite (0.00). Requires Dirichlet integral---frontier-only capability.

\paragraph{Case 3: Efficiency Opportunity.} \emph{``Why do birds chirp in the morning?''} $\to$ Nova-Lite (0.78) over GPT-4o (0.75). Near-equal quality triggers 97.7\% cheaper routing.

\paragraph{Aggregate.} Across 497 clusters: 5.6\% Specialist Wins, 7.0\% Teacher Fallbacks, 45.5\% Efficiency Opportunities. The router's primary value: 97.6\% cost savings on nearly half of traffic where expensive models add no quality.
