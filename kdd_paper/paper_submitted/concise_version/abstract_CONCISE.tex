\begin{abstract}
Large language models remain inaccessible due to dual barriers: frontier models cost \$4--15 per 1,000 queries at late-2024 on-demand prices~\cite{openai2025pricing,anthropic2025pricing}, creating \emph{economic barriers} that exclude students, researchers, and startups. While more than 80 specialized models on public marketplaces offer up to $\sim$90\% cost reduction, with prices as low as \$0.10--0.88 per 1,000 queries~\cite{openrouter2025models,artificialanalysis2025}, practitioners lack accessible tools to navigate this fragmentation, resulting in systematic over-provisioning or abandoned optimization.

We present \ours{}, an open-source routing framework that eliminates both barriers via \emph{shippable priors}---compact covariance matrices (<1MB) distilled from offline supervision. Users deploy in minutes without labeled data, achieving 96--99\% day-one regret reduction versus cold-start. Learning from FrugalGPT's cascading reliability and RouteLLM's preference routing, we address their calibration bottlenecks: users add new models via 30-second registration (vs.\ 1--3 days benchmarking), specify constraints intuitively (\texttt{max\_budget=\$50}, \texttt{min\_quality=90\%}), and benefit from autonomous online adaptation.

\textbf{Standard mode} reduces costs 61\% versus FrugalGPT (\$0.70 vs.\ \$1.78/1k in our simulation) despite zero calibration requirements. \textbf{Hybrid mode} achieves 98\% reliability (3pp over cascading baselines) across 80+ models without manual chain design. Routing overhead is 8.94ms P99 (1.1\% of latency). By releasing pre-trained priors and intuitive control interfaces, we expand adaptive routing from ML specialists to general programmers---an order-of-magnitude expansion ($\approx 15$--$25\times$) in the addressable developer base~\cite{stackoverflow2024survey,github2025developer}---demonstrating that democratizing AI access requires addressing operational complexity alongside cost.
\end{abstract}
