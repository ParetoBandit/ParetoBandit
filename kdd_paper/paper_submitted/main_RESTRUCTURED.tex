% ABSTRACT (REVISED v2 - Democratization Focus)
% ============================================================================
\begin{abstract}
The transformative potential of Large Language Models remains largely inaccessible due to two compounding barriers. Frontier models cost \$4--15 per 1,000 queries, creating economic barriers for students, researchers, and startups. Simultaneously, existing routing tools require operational barriers: 500--2,000 labeled examples for calibration, domain-specific scorer training, and days of setup time---resources unavailable to users without ML infrastructure. While the ecosystem has fragmented into 80+ models offering cost-efficient alternatives (\$0.10/1k), practitioners lack accessible tools to navigate this complexity. The result is systematic over-provisioning: users default to expensive models out of uncertainty, paying 10--100× more than necessary, or abandon adaptive routing entirely due to operational complexity.

We present \ours{}, an open-source routing framework that removes both barriers through operational innovation. By distilling offline expert supervision into \emph{shippable priors}---compact covariance matrices under 1MB---we enable production-ready routing from the first query, eliminating calibration requirements entirely. Users download pre-trained priors and deploy in minutes without labeled data, scorer training, or manual chain design. Our framework achieves a 96--99\% reduction in day-one regret with zero user-provided calibration data, while preserving adaptive plasticity to handle evolving model capabilities through autonomous online learning.

Learning from FrugalGPT's strength in cascading reliability and RouteLLM's preference-based routing, we address their accessibility limitations to serve users who lack ML expertise or labeled datasets. \ours{} establishes a complementary Pareto frontier: \textbf{Standard mode} reduces inference costs by 61\% compared to FrugalGPT (\$0.70 vs.\ \$1.78 per 1k queries) despite eliminating calibration requirements, enabling high-volume experimentation for budget-constrained users. \textbf{Hybrid mode} achieves 98\% reliability on instruction-following tasks (outperforming cascading baselines by 3 percentage points) while operating over an 80+ model pool without manual chain configuration. A single tunable parameter ($\lambda$) enables smooth navigation between ``Cost Leader'' and ``High Assurance'' operational regimes, supporting diverse user needs from educational projects to production deployments.

Evaluation on 2,000 real-world prompts across 81 production models demonstrates that operational simplicity does not sacrifice performance: strategic routing delivers comparable or superior quality to existing systems (95--98\% accuracy) while reducing both economic barriers (61--84\% cost reduction) and expertise barriers (minutes vs.\ days of setup). Routing overhead is 8.94ms P99 (1.1\% of total latency), making cost savings effectively free. By releasing \ours{} as open-source infrastructure with pre-trained priors, we expand adaptive routing from ML specialists (requiring labeled data and domain expertise) to general programmers (requiring only basic Python skills), increasing the accessible user base by an estimated 25×. This work demonstrates that democratizing AI access requires addressing not only cost, but also the operational complexity that keeps proven techniques confined to specialized teams.
\end{abstract}
