\subsection{RQ4: Does Semantic Transfer Improve Cold-Start Routing?}
\label{sec:semantic_transfer}

We evaluate whether the updated encoder (BAAI/bge-m3) and 15-component PCA representation enable meaningful model-to-model transfer when a new model joins the portfolio. Using a leave-one-out design over the $K{=}3$ portfolio (Llama-3.1-8B / Gemini-2.5-Flash / GPT-4.1), each model is treated in turn as a simulated newcomer. The remaining $K{-}1$ models receive canonical warmup priors; the newcomer is initialised either by \emph{semantic transfer} from its nearest within-provider neighbor (Condition~A) or from a tabula rasa identity prior (Condition~B). Neighbor selection uses within-provider tetrachoric correlation of binarised reward vectors, matching the library's \texttt{compute\_correlation\_families()} design.
