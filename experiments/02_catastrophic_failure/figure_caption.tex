\textbf{Catastrophic failure: Normal $\to$ Mistral failure $\to$ Recovered
(K=3, 20 seeds, 95\% bootstrap CI).}
\textbf{(a)}~Gemini-Pro selection fraction: the unconstrained baseline freely
shifts ${\sim}80\%$ of traffic to expensive Gemini-Pro when Mistral fails, while
budget-constrained conditions remain near zero---forced to absorb the failure
via cheaper Llama-8B.
\textbf{(b)}~Windowed mean reward (trailing 50~steps): the Gemini gap in~(a)
explains the quality gap---unconstrained recovers to ${\sim}0.9$ during failure
while constrained conditions dip more because Llama is lower quality.
\textbf{(c)}~Windowed average cost per request; dotted lines mark budget targets.
All three budget regimes maintain compliance ($0.97$--$1.03\times$) throughout
the failure, while unconstrained cost spikes.
See Table~\ref{tab:failure_response} for quantitative comparisons across algorithms.
