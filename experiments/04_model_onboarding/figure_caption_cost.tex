\textbf{Budget--quality trade-off during model onboarding (Good \& Cheap).}
\textbf{(a)}~Running average cost per request for three budget tiers
(dotted lines = targets).
Tight and moderate track their ceilings to within 1\% throughout the
K=3 $\to$ K=4 transition.
The loose tier settles ${\sim}6$\% below its target---a case of
\emph{complementary slackness}: with only four arms and a
$29{\times}$ cost gap between Mistral (\$5.1e-4/req) and Gemini-Pro
(\$1.5e-2/req), the quality-maximising allocation at ${\sim}8$\%
Pro naturally costs \$1.71e-3, below the \$1.87e-3 ceiling.
The dual variable confirms this: $\lambda_t \approx 0.06$ (loose)
vs.\ $0.41$ (tight), indicating the constraint is slack at the
quality optimum.
\textbf{(b)}~Cumulative average reward.
Tight budget reward dips ${\sim}1$\% at the Phase~2 boundary---the
bounded cost of 20 forced-exploration pulls ($1.1$\% of Phase~2
traffic).
Moderate reward \emph{rises} by $1$\% after onboarding: Flash
($\bar{r}{=}0.925$, \$8.8e-4/req) fills a quality niche previously
inaccessible at that budget tier, replacing prompts that would have
gone to lower-quality Llama.
Loose and unconstrained rewards are nearly identical, confirming the
budget constraint is non-binding.
20~seeds; shaded regions = 95\% bootstrap CI.
