\textbf{Model onboarding under budget constraints (K=3 $\to$ K=4).}
A K=3 router (Llama-8B, Mistral-Large, Gemini-Pro) learns on 1{,}785
validation prompts with warmup priors, then onboards Gemini-2.5-Flash
as a fourth arm via \texttt{register\_model()} (dashed vertical line).
The first 20 Phase~2 prompts are routed unconditionally to Flash
(forced exploration; dotted orange line), after which UCB selection
takes over on the K=4 test split (1{,}793 prompts).
Each panel overlays Flash's windowed fraction across four budget
regimes.
\textbf{(a)}~Good \& Cheap (baseline rewards and costs): Flash is
adopted across all budgets, reaching ${\sim}10$\% share under loose
constraints.
\textbf{(b)}~Good \& Expensive (costs scaled $10\times$): the
BudgetPacer suppresses Flash under tight and moderate budgets but
permits partial adoption (${\sim}6$\%) when unconstrained.
\textbf{(c)}~Bad \& Cheap (rewards scaled $0.5\times$): the bandit
correctly rejects Flash in every seed and budget tier, with final
share dropping to 0\% after burn-in.
95\% bootstrap CI across 20~seeds.
