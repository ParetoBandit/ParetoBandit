\textbf{Model onboarding under budget constraints (K=3 $\to$ K=4).}
A K=3 router (Llama-8B, Mistral-Large, Gemini-Pro) learns on 1{,}785
prompts with warmup priors, then onboards Gemini-2.5-Flash as a fourth arm
via \texttt{register\_model()} (dashed vertical line).
Phase~2 continues online on the K=4 test split (1{,}824 prompts).
\textbf{(a)}~Flash adoption trajectory: the windowed selection fraction
of Flash rises from zero after onboarding and stabilises within
${\sim}230$ steps across all budget tiers. Loose budgets reach
${\sim}38$\% Flash share; tight budgets plateau at ${\sim}14$\%.
The grey dotted line marks 25\% (uniform~1/4 baseline).
\textbf{(b)}~Full arm composition under the moderate budget: Flash
primarily displaces Mistral-Large ($-22.9$\,pp), taking over the
mid-tier quality niche at lower cost.
\textbf{(c)}~Cost compliance on log scale: ParetoBandit (solid) tracks
each budget target (dotted) within ${\sim}2$\% across the full
trajectory, while the Fixed Policy (dashed grey) exceeds all targets
by 2--14$\times$.
95\% bootstrap CI across 20~seeds.
