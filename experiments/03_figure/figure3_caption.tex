    \caption{Model onboarding: adding Mistral-Large-2512 to a $K{=}2$
    portfolio (Llama-3.1-8B + Gemini-2.5-Pro) via a single
    \texttt{register\_model()} call.
    %
    \textbf{Top left (Hybrid LinUCB):}\ the shared $\hat{\beta}$
    from $K{=}2$ training transfers to the newcomer, giving it
    an initial quality prediction of $\hat{r} \approx 0.76$.
    Mistral reaches $52\%$ cumulative routing share and Gemini
    collapses to $17\%$ (CostSave@95\% $= 79\%$).
    %
    \textbf{Top right (Disjoint LinUCB, ablation):}\ without the
    shared $\hat{\beta}$, Mistral starts with $\hat{r} = 0$ and the
    UCB exploration bonus at $\alpha{=}0.05$ cannot overcome the
    incumbents' advantage---routing share remains at exactly $0\%$
    across all seeds (cold-start trap; CostSave@95\% $= 36\%$).
    %
    \textbf{Bottom (reward overlay):}\ cumulative average reward
    for both conditions.
    Hybrid's reward rises after onboarding; Disjoint stays flat.
    %
    Both conditions share identical $K{=}2$ warmup priors for the
    original arms; only the shared $\hat{\beta}$ differs.
    No artificial exploration boost is applied.
    The Corralling meta-learner uses an adaptive $\gamma$ schedule:
    $\gamma$ drops to~0 upon \texttt{register\_model()} and ramps
    linearly back to~0.05 over 500~steps, giving the warm-started
    expert full control during the critical discovery window.
    Setup: $n{=}8{,}374$ training prompts ($K{=}2$),
    $n{=}1{,}824$ test prompts ($K{=}3$),
    $\alpha{=}0.05$, $\neff{=}50$, averaged over 20~seeds
    with SE bands.
    }
    \label{fig:onboarding_k3}
