    dev-train split with hyperparameters tuned on the same dev-val split

    hyperparameters from Appendix~H) is swept over $\lambda \in [0,\,5]$
    (23 values).
    Frozen holdout evaluation with greedy exploitation ($\alpha{=}0$).
    Error bars show 95\% $t$-CIs across 20 seeds.
    In this two-model setting, all quality-maximising strategies
    converge to the best single model (GPT-4.1); BanditGPT's advantage
    is the continuous cost--quality tradeoff via~$\lambda$.

    \textbf{(b)}~Learning curve: holdout reward as a function of online
    training steps ($\lambda{=}0$, quality-only).  BanditGPT's step-0
    performance reflects warmup priors (transfer learning from a broader
    model registry); supervised baselines are trained on the full dev-train
    split and shown as horizontal reference lines.
    %
    Rewards are ground-truth multi-judge scores (mean of vote $\times$
    confidence via \texttt{extract\_reward}), not proxy predictions.}
