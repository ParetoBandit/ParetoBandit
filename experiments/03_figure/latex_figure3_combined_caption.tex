    \caption{%
        \textbf{Prior Degradation Sweep} ($N{=}20$ seeds, $\pm 1\sigma$ bands).
        Corralling achieves the lowest regret for good priors ($\alpha \leq 0.2$, regret 48--51), beating warmup-only (52--58) and tabula rasa (${\sim}51.3$).
        Beyond the crossover ($\alpha{\approx}0.3$), Corralling's regret rises sharply (65--75, $\sigma{\approx}23$), while tabula rasa remains flat regardless of corruption.
        Warmup-only degrades monotonically (52 to 67) and is never optimal.
        Setup: 750 LMSYS prompts, 2 models, $\eta{\approx}0.030$, $\gamma{=}0.05$, $w_0{=}0.7$, warmup $\alpha{=}2.0$, tabula rasa $\alpha{=}1.0 \to 0.01$, $\lambda{=}0$.
