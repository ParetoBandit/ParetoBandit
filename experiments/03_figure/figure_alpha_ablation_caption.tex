    \caption{Sensitivity of the learning curve to the exploration coefficient $\alpha$
    (K=2, $\lambda{=}0$, $n_{\mathrm{eff}}{=}5000$, 20 seeds).
    %
    In the Corralling router, $\alpha$ controls the UCB bonus:
    the warmup expert uses constant $\alpha$ while the tabula-rasa expert
    decays from $2\alpha$ to $0.01$.
    %
    Higher $\alpha$ ($\geq 2.0$) produces a characteristic peak-then-dip artifact:
    strong prior-informed decisions in the first ${\sim}25$ steps
    followed by degradation as noisy online updates corrupt the prior,
    amplified by the large exploration bonus.
    Lower $\alpha$ avoids this artifact, yielding a smooth monotonic learning curve.
    %
    Converged performance is robust to $\alpha$, and all values surpass
    RouteLLM's dev-selected quality ($0.783$, dashed line).
    The production value $\alpha{=}1.0$ is selected via the joint
    Appendix~H grid ablation.}
    \label{fig:alpha_ablation}
\end{figure}
