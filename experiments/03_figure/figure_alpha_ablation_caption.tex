    \includegraphics[width=0.7\linewidth]{figures/figure_alpha_ablation.png}
    \caption{Sensitivity of the learning curve to the exploration coefficient $\alpha$
    (K=2, $\lambda{=}0$, 20 seeds).
    %
    In the hybrid Corralling router, $\alpha$ controls the UCB bonus:
    the warmup expert uses constant $\alpha$ while the tabula-rasa expert
    decays from $\alpha/2$ to $0.01$.
    %
    Higher $\alpha$ ($\geq 1.0$) produces a characteristic peak-then-dip artifact:
    strong prior-informed decisions in the first ${\sim}25$ steps
    followed by degradation as noisy online updates corrupt the prior,
    amplified by the large exploration bonus.
    Lower $\alpha$ ($0.5$) avoids this artifact entirely, yielding a smooth
    monotonic learning curve.
    %
    Converged performance (step $1{,}121$) is robust to $\alpha$
    ($0.811$--$0.816$), and all values surpass RouteLLM's peak ($0.788$,
    dashed line).
    $\alpha{=}0.5$ achieves the highest final reward ($0.816 \pm 0.007$)
    and is adopted as the default.}
    \label{fig:alpha_ablation}
\end{figure}
