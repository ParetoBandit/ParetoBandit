    \caption{Budget pacing under cost drift ($K{=}3$; 50~seeds,
    $\pm$1\,SE shading).
    %
    \textbf{Top row: BanditGPT adaptation mechanics.}
    %
    \textbf{(a)~Dual variable $\lambda_t$:}
    At moderate budget, $\lambda_t$ drops from ${\sim}0.35$ to
    ${\sim}0.01$ as post-drop costs fall below target;
    at tight budget, $\lambda_t$ \emph{rises} from $0.46$ to $0.82$
    because the constraint remains binding.
    %
    \textbf{(b)~Gemini-Pro selection fraction:}
    At tight budget, Gemini surges to ${\sim}84\%$---the elevated
    $\lambda_t$ penalizes the now-expensive Mistral while Gemini's
    near-zero cost is barely affected.
    %
    \textbf{(c)~Running average cost:}
    BanditGPT tracks the budget target (dotted lines) within $3\%$.
    %
    \textbf{Bottom row: All-condition budget compliance.}
    \textbf{(d--f)} Running average cost for all four conditions plus
    the unconstrained baseline, at tight, moderate, and loose budget.
    The black dotted line marks the budget target.
    BanditGPT (solid orange) is the only condition that tracks the
    target across both phases; the Recalibrated Bandit (dash-dot blue)
    re-tunes $\lambda_s$ at the Phase~2 boundary but inherits Phase~1
    non-compliance and cannot adjust within Phase~2.
