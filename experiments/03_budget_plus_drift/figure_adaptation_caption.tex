\begin{figure*}[htbp]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../experiments/03_budget_plus_drift/results/adaptation_dynamics.pdf}
    \caption{BanditGPT adaptation dynamics under a Gemini price drop at
    three budget levels ($K{=}3$; Pacer + $\gamma{=}0.995$; 50~seeds,
    $\pm$1\,SE shading).  The unconstrained BanditGPT baseline (same
    algorithm, same priors, no budget constraint) is shown in green
    dash-dot for comparison.
    %
    \textbf{(a)~Dual variable $\lambda_t$:}
    At moderate budget, $\lambda_t$ drops from ${\sim}0.35$ to
    ${\sim}0.01$ within Phase~2 of the price drop
    (step~912).  At loose budget, $\lambda_t$ was already near zero.
    At tight budget, $\lambda_t$ averages $0.45$ in Phase~1 and
    remains elevated at $0.43$ due to EMA momentum.
    %
    \textbf{(b)~Gemini-Pro selection fraction:}
    At moderate and loose budgets, Gemini selection surges from
    $2$--$10\%$ to ${\sim}45$--$47\%$ after the price drop, approaching the
    unconstrained level (${\sim}75\%$).  At tight budget, Gemini
    remains near $0\%$ due to exploration starvation
    (Section~\ref{sec:budget_drift}).
    %
    \textbf{(c)~Running average cost per request:}
    BanditGPT maintains Phase~1 costs within $3\%$ of the budget
    target (dotted horizontal lines).  Post-drop, costs decline below
    target.  The unconstrained baseline spends
    ${\sim}6$--$51\times$ more.
    }
    \label{fig:exp03_adaptation}
\end{figure*}
