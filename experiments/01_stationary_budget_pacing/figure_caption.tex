    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../experiments/01_stationary_budget_pacing/results/pareto_frontier.pdf}
    \caption{Budget-constrained routing under stationary conditions
    ($K{=}3$: \texttt{Llama-3.1-8B}, \texttt{Mistral-Large-2512},
    \texttt{Gemini-2.5-Pro}; 20~seeds, 95\% bootstrap CI error bars).
    %
    The \textbf{BudgetPacer} (blue, solid) implements a primal-dual
    Contextual Bandit with Knapsacks
    (CBwK)~\cite{agrawal2014bandits} that accepts a
    dollar-denominated budget target $B$ and adaptively tunes a
    Lagrange multiplier $\lambda_t$ via projected gradient ascent on
    an EMA-smoothed cost signal
    (Eq.~\ref{eq:dual_update}).
    $\lambda_t$ simultaneously tightens a hard cost ceiling
    (filtering out models whose unit price exceeds
    $p_{\max}/(1 + \kappa\lambda_t)$)
    and injects a soft per-model penalty
    $\lambda_t \cdot \tilde{c}_m$ into the LinUCB~\cite{li2010contextual}
    score, where $\tilde{c}_m \in [0,1]$ is the log-normalized cost.
    %
    The \textbf{static cost penalty} baseline (gray, dashed) applies a
    fixed dimensionless weight $\lambda_s \in \{0, 0.05, \ldots, 1.0\}$
    to the same normalized cost; each point on the curve requires an
    offline sweep to identify the $\lambda_s$ that achieves a given
    dollar spend.
    %
    Marker fill indicates budget compliance for pacer points:
    \textcolor[HTML]{009E73}{\textbf{green}} = on-target
    ($0.95$--$1.05\times$),
    \textcolor[HTML]{E69F00}{\textbf{orange}} = under-target
    ($< 0.95\times$).
    %
    The pacer achieves near-perfect budget compliance
    (utilisation $0.97$--$1.02\times$) for the five tightest targets
    while matching or exceeding the static baseline's reward at
    comparable cost.
    At the loosest target the pacer under-spends because the
    unconstrained router's natural mean cost
    (\$0.0094/req) is below the target---the dual variable correctly
    decays to $\lambda_t \approx 0$ and the router operates
    unconstrained.
    }
    \label{fig:pareto_budget}
\end{figure*}
