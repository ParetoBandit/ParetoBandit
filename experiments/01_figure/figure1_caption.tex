    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{../experiments/01_figure/results/figure1_pareto_k2.pdf}
    \caption{Cost--quality Pareto frontier for \textit{banditGPT}
    on a two-model portfolio (\texttt{Llama-3.1-8B} at
    \$0.00003/req vs.\ \texttt{Gemini-2.5-Pro} at \$0.015/req;
    $500{\times}$ cost ratio).
    %
    \textbf{Solid blue curve:}\ bandit frontier traced by sweeping
    the cost penalty $\lambda$ over 22 values in $[0,\,10]$, averaged
    over 5~independent seeds (different train/test shuffle orders).
    The shaded band shows $\pm 1$~SE across seeds.
    Each $\lambda$ produces a different cost--quality operating
    point~\cite{chen2023frugalgpt, li2026llmrouterbench};
    the bandit continues learning during the test phase, faithfully
    reflecting online deployment~\cite{li2010contextual}.
    %
    \textbf{Dashed gray line:}\ static random-mix baseline---the
    convex combination of the two fixed-model endpoints, corresponding
    to routing each request to \texttt{Llama} with probability~$p$
    and \texttt{Gemini} with probability~$1{-}p$ \emph{without}
    conditioning on the prompt.  This is the best a non-contextual
    policy can achieve.
    %
    \textbf{Star markers:}\ CostSave\,@\,$Q$ operating points at
    quality thresholds $Q \in \{90\%,\, 95\%,\, 99\%\}$.
    CostSave~\cite{li2026llmrouterbench, chen2023frugalgpt} is
    the percentage reduction in cost the bandit frontier achieves
    relative to the frontier-model cost at matched quality
    ($Q \times R_{\text{strong}}$).  The advantage $\Delta$ (in
    percentage points) is the bandit saving minus the static-baseline
    saving at the same quality level.  95\% confidence intervals are
    from a prompt-level paired
    bootstrap~\cite{efron1993bootstrap}
    ($n{=}1{,}824$ test prompts,
    $B{=}2{,}000$ resamples); the same resampled indices are applied to
    both the bandit and static baselines so the CI is on the
    \emph{advantage}, not the absolute saving.
    %
    Setup: train $n{=}8{,}374$, test $n{=}1{,}824$ prompts scored by
    DeepSeek-R1~\cite{deepseekai2025deepseekr1}
    (LLM-as-judge~\cite{zheng2024llmjudge} proxy for implicit
    user feedback;
    see Section~\ref{sec:reward_assumption}).
    Hyperparameters ($\alpha{=}1.0$, $\neff{=}10$, hybrid
    LinUCB~\cite{li2010contextual})
    selected on a held-out validation split.
    }
    \label{fig:pareto_k2}
\end{figure}
