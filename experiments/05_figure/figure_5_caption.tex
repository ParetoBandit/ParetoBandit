\caption{\textbf{Corralling enables automatic recovery from catastrophic LLM failure.}
%
Three-phase scenario on the $K{=}3$ portfolio using \textbf{real prompts,
embeddings, and rewards} (20 seeds).  The online routing stream uses
dev-train prompts ($80\%$ of the online-learn pool, excluding the
prior-train set); the holdout set is reserved for periodic frozen
evaluation.  Each phase cycles through the full dev-train set:
all models healthy, GPT-4.1 (best static model) catastrophically fails
to near-zero quality, then recovers.  All rewards except the failing
model's during Phase~2 are real ground-truth evaluations.
%
\textbf{(a)}~Running-average reward ($w{=}20$) with $\pm 1\sigma$ bands.
BanditGPT (Corralling + warmup priors) maintains high quality during the
failure phase by redistributing traffic, while the static baseline collapses.
The warmup-only ablation (no Corralling) cannot adapt because it trusts the
now-stale priors.
%
\textbf{(b)}~Corralling expert weights.  When GPT-4.1 fails, the meta-learner
shifts probability mass from the warmup expert (whose priors still favour
GPT-4.1) to the tabula rasa expert (which learns the new reward landscape
from scratch).  Upon recovery, the warmup expert's weight is restored.
%
\textbf{(c)}~BanditGPT model selection fractions.  During the failure phase,
traffic is automatically redirected away from GPT-4.1 toward the remaining
models, with context-dependent routing across the $K{-}1$ healthy alternatives.
%
Per-expert hyperparameters from
Appendix~\ref{sec:alpha_neff_ablation}; 3-model portfolio-specific
warmup priors.  Cost penalty $\lambda{=}0$ (quality-only).}
\Description{Three-panel figure: (a) reward time series with phase shading showing BanditGPT maintains quality during failure, (b) Corralling expert weights shifting during failure and recovering, (c) model selection fractions showing traffic redistribution away from the failed model.}
\label{fig:catastrophic_failure}
