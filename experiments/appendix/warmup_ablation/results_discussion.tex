\subsection{Cold-Start vs Warmup Priors (Figure~\ref{fig:warmup_ablation})}
\label{sec:warmup_ablation}

We isolate the contribution of warmup priors by comparing
BanditGPT with offline priors against a tabula-rasa cold start
(identity covariance, zero bias) using identical hyperparameters
($\alpha{=}1.0$, $n_{\text{eff}}{=}5{,}000$, disjoint LinUCB,
$\gamma{=}1.0$) on the $K{=}3$ stationary portfolio.

\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrr@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Regret} (95\% CI) & \textbf{Regret@200} (95\% CI) & \textbf{Mean Rwd} & \textbf{Oracle Agr.} \\
\midrule
BanditGPT (warmup) & $\mathbf{58.0}$\,[57.2, 58.8] & $\mathbf{5.6}$\,[5.1, 6.0]  & $\mathbf{0.931}$ & $\mathbf{0.662}$ \\
Tabula Rasa        & $90.1$\,[89.0, 91.2]         & $14.1$\,[13.3, 14.9]        & $0.913$           & $0.644$           \\
Random             & $145.3$\,[142.8, 147.6]      & $16.3$\,[15.5, 17.1]        & $0.882$           & $0.528$           \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
Warmup priors reduce total regret by 36\% ($\Delta{=}-32.1$,
paired $t$-test $p < 10^{-18}$) and early regret (step~200) by
61\%.  The advantage is concentrated in the first ${\sim}400$
steps (Figure~\ref{fig:warmup_ablation}), where the warmup router
begins with informed arm-quality estimates and avoids the
exploratory phase that the cold-start router must perform.
Beyond step~${\sim}800$, the cold-start router has accumulated
enough observations to converge, but the regret gap established
early persists through the full horizon.
Twenty seeds; 95\% bootstrap CI shading; val split ($n{=}1{,}785$).

\paragraph{Uncertainty evolution (Figure~\ref{fig:warmup_uncertainty}).}
To visualise the mechanism behind this regret gap, Figure~\ref{fig:warmup_uncertainty} plots $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1})$---the total uncertainty per arm---for both conditions across the trajectory.
Warmup-initialised arms (solid lines) start at $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1}) \approx 0.15$, reflecting the $n_\mathit{eff}{=}5{,}000$ pseudo-observations encoded in the offline prior.
Cold-started arms (dashed lines) begin at $\mathrm{tr}(A_a^{-1}) = d / \lambda = 26$---a ${\sim}150{\times}$ uncertainty gap.
Because the UCB exploration bonus scales as $\alpha\sqrt{x^\top A_a^{-1} x}$, this gap means the cold-start router must spend hundreds of steps exploring before it accumulates enough information to route as confidently as the warmup router does from step~1.
Even at step~1{,}785 the cold-start lines remain above the warmup lines, confirming that the information advantage of offline priors is \emph{not} fully recovered within the trajectory length, consistent with the persistent regret gap in Figure~\ref{fig:warmup_ablation}.
