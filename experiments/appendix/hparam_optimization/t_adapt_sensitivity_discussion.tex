% Discussion: T_adapt Sensitivity Analysis
% Include from appendix section

\paragraph{$T_{\text{adapt}}$ sensitivity.}
A natural concern is whether the selected configuration is sensitive
to the anchor value $T_{\text{adapt}}{=}\hpTAdapt$, which was set to
approximately match the catastrophic-failure phase length.
To test robustness, we repeat the full Pareto knee-point selection
for $T_{\text{adapt}} \in \{250, 500, 1000\}$---a $4{\times}$ range
that spans reaction times from rapid detection to conservative
adaptation.
Three observations support robustness:
%
\begin{enumerate}[nosep,leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{$\alpha$ is perfectly stable}
    ($\hpSensMidAlpha$ at every $T_{\text{adapt}}$), as expected:
    $\alpha$ is orthogonal to the adaptation horizon.

  \item \textbf{$\gamma$ shifts but stays in the moderate-forgetting region.}
    The selected values---$\hpSensLoGamma$ ($T{=}250$),
    $\hpSensMidGamma$ ($T{=}500$), $\hpSensHiGamma$
    ($T{=}1000$)---span three grid steps
    ($\Delta\gamma = 0.003$), with derived
    $n_{\text{eff}}$ of $\hpSensLoNeff$, $\hpSensMidNeff$, and
    $\hpSensHiNeff$ respectively.
    The direction is expected: a larger $T_{\text{adapt}}$ derives a
    larger $n_{\text{eff}}$, and the Pareto criterion compensates
    with more aggressive forgetting.
    Crucially, every selected $\gamma$ remains well below the
    $\gamma{=}1.0$ regime where forgetting is absent, so the
    qualitative behaviour---moderate discounting with
    non-stationary resilience---is preserved regardless of
    the $T_{\text{adapt}}$ anchor.

  \item \textbf{Both objectives are effectively invariant.}
    Despite the three-step $\gamma$ shift,
    AUC varies by $<$\,0.25\% ($\hpSensLoAUC$--$\hpSensMidAUC$)
    and Phase-2 reward spans only
    $\hpSensMidPTwo$--$\hpSensHiPTwo$, with no monotonic trend.
    The performance surface near the Pareto knee is flat, so the
    choice of $T_{\text{adapt}}$ affects the internal
    parameterisation ($\gamma$, $n_{\text{eff}}$) more than the
    external routing outcomes.
\end{enumerate}
%
A practitioner can therefore set $T_{\text{adapt}}$ to their
operational requirement: while the internal $\gamma$ and
$n_{\text{eff}}$ will adjust, the resulting routing performance
is effectively unchanged.
