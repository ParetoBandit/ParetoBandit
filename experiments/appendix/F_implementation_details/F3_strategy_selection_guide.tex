\textbf{Validation method:} 
Evaluate warmup predictions on N=100--200 deployment samples. 
Accuracy $>$80\% = good priors; 50--80\% = uncertain; $<$50\% = bad priors.

\textbf{Key findings:}
(1) Corralling provides 18.5\% safety vs harmful warmup (59.2 vs 74.7);
(2) Tabula Rasa achieves 16\% improvement when priors known bad (49.5 vs 59.2);
(3) Adaptive decay degrades performance by 48\% (90.2 regret) under mismatch.
