\section{Algorithm Pseudocode}
\label{appendix:pseudocode}

Algorithm~\ref{alg:paretobandit} (Section~\ref{sec:methodology}) gives the complete per-step routing loop.  Algorithm~\ref{alg:warmup} below details the offline-to-online warmup prior initialization referenced on line~1 of Algorithm~\ref{alg:paretobandit}.

\begin{algorithm}[t]
\caption{Offline-to-Online Warmup Prior Initialization}
\label{alg:warmup}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Offline sufficient statistics $\{(A_a^{\mathrm{off}}, b_a^{\mathrm{off}})\}_{a \in \mathcal{A}}$; target prior strength $n_\mathit{eff}$; regularization $\lambda_0$
\For{each arm $a \in \mathcal{A}$}
    \If{offline prior exists for $a$}
        \State $\hat\theta_a^{\mathrm{off}} \leftarrow (A_a^{\mathrm{off}})^{-1}\, b_a^{\mathrm{off}}$ \Comment{Offline reward estimate}
        \State $s \leftarrow n_\mathit{eff}\, \big/\, A_a^{\mathrm{off}}[d, d]$ \Comment{Scale to target strength}
        \State $A_a \leftarrow s \cdot A_a^{\mathrm{off}} + \lambda_0 I$ \Comment{Scaled prior + regularization}
        \State $b_a \leftarrow s \cdot b_a^{\mathrm{off}} + \lambda_0\, \hat\theta_a^{\mathrm{off}}$ \Comment{Preserve prior mean}
    \Else \Comment{Cold-start heuristic}
        \State $A_a \leftarrow n_\mathit{eff} \cdot I + \lambda_0 I$
        \State $b_a \leftarrow \mathbf{0}$;\; $b_a[d] \leftarrow q_a \cdot (n_\mathit{eff} + \lambda_0)$ \Comment{$q_a$: quality estimate}
    \EndIf
    \State $A_a^{-1} \leftarrow (A_a)^{-1}$;\;\; $\hat\theta_a \leftarrow A_a^{-1}\, b_a$
\EndFor
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The scaling factor $s$ (line~4) normalizes the offline precision mass to match the target $n_\mathit{eff}$, using the bias-direction diagonal $A_a^{\mathrm{off}}[d, d]$ as a proxy for the total number of effective offline observations.  Adding $\lambda_0 I$ (line~5) provides Tikhonov regularization for numerical stability.  The bias correction on line~6---adding $\lambda_0\, \hat\theta_a^{\mathrm{off}}$ to $b_a$---ensures that the regularization does not shrink the posterior mean toward zero: it preserves $A_a^{-1} b_a \approx \hat\theta_a^{\mathrm{off}}$ so that the prior encodes the correct reward estimate at the desired confidence level.

For models absent from the offline data (lines~7--9), a heuristic prior places $n_\mathit{eff}$ pseudo-observations at isotropic uncertainty with a bias-only reward prediction equal to the model's estimated quality $q_a$ (drawn from registry metadata or a default).
