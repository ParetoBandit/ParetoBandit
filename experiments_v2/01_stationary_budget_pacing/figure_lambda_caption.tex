    \caption{Dual-variable trajectory $\lambda_t$ over the 1{,}824-step
    test phase (seed~0) at three representative budget targets, all
    with binding constraints (utilisation $\geq 0.98\times$).
    %
    \textbf{Top} (tight, $B{=}\$2.9{\times}10^{-5}$/req):
    $\lambda_t$ saturates near the cap $\bar\lambda{=}5$, forcing
    near-exclusive Llama routing.  The hard ceiling filters
    Mistral and Gemini outright; the soft penalty is redundant.
    Budget utilisation: $1.07\times$; $\lambda_{\mathrm{final}}{=}4.89$.
    %
    \textbf{Middle} (moderate, $B{=}\$6.6{\times}10^{-4}$/req):
    $\lambda_t$ oscillates in a bounded band ($q_{25}{=}0.09$,
    $q_{50}{=}0.20$, $q_{75}{=}0.39$), actively steering a
    Llama/Mistral/Gemini mix.  The oscillations reflect the
    EMA-smoothed cost responding to stochastic model selections:
    expensive requests raise $\bar{c}_t$ above target, driving
    $\lambda_t$ up, which suppresses costly selections, allowing
    $\bar{c}_t$ to relax.  The bounded amplitude confirms the
    primal-dual controller is orbiting the constraint boundary
    in steady state.
    Budget utilisation: $1.00\times$; $\lambda_{\mathrm{final}}{=}0.19$.
    %
    \textbf{Bottom} (loose-binding, $B{=}\$0.0053$/req):
    $\lambda_t$ applies light pressure ($q_{50}{=}0.002$),
    with occasional small adjustments when the cost EMA
    drifts above target.  The gentle touch preserves nearly
    unconstrained quality ($0.916$) while enforcing $0.98\times$
    utilisation.
    %
    The 50-step moving average (solid line) shows the smoothed trend;
    the dotted horizontal line marks $\lambda_{\mathrm{final}}$.
    Using the EMA-smoothed cost $\bar{c}_t$ (rather than the raw
    per-request cost $c_t$) in the dual update eliminates the
    high-variance sawtooth oscillations that would otherwise arise
    from model costs spanning $500\times$.
    %
    (20~seeds; trajectory shown for seed~0.)
    }
    \label{fig:lambda_convergence}
\end{figure}
