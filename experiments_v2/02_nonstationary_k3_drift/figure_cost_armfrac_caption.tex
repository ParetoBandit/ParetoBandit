    \caption{Model selection dynamics under Gemini price drop
    ($K{=}3$; BanditGPT $\gamma{=}0.999$, 40~seeds, $\pm$1\,SD shading).
    %
    Each line shows the fraction of holdout prompts routed to each
    model at every checkpoint, evaluated via frozen
    \texttt{select\_arm()} with Phase~2 normalized costs.
    %
    Fixed $\gamma{=}0.999$ converges to 59\% Gemini by
    step~1{,}785---below the No~forgetting baseline's 95\%
    (Figure~\ref{fig:cost_shift_regret})---because the forgetting
    factor decays the Phase~1 $\hat\theta$ estimates, increasing
    uncertainty across \emph{all} arms and driving exploration
    that is unnecessary given the immediate cost signal.
    %
    The adaptive-$\gamma$ condition mitigates this: by largely
    suppressing forgetting when prediction residuals remain low
    (Figure~\ref{fig:gamma_trajectory}), it reaches 69\% Gemini
    at termination---a 10 percentage point improvement over fixed
    $\gamma{=}0.999$ and a significant regret reduction
    ($\Delta{=}{-}11.1$, $p < 10^{-12}$;
    Figure~\ref{fig:cost_shift_regret}).
