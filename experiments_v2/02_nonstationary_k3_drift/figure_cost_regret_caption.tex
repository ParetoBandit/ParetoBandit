    \caption{Cumulative cost-adjusted regret under Gemini price drop
    ($K{=}3$; 40~seeds, $\pm$1\,SE shading).
    %
    At step~893, Gemini's pricing drops from \$1.25/\$10.0 to
    \$0.10/\$0.10 per million tokens (input/output), reducing its
    normalized cost from $0.67$ to ${\approx}0.0$.  Post-drop,
    Gemini becomes utility-best
    ($0.942 = 0.933 - 0.20 \times 0.0$ vs.\ Mistral's $0.845$).
    %
    Unlike the reward swap (Figure~\ref{fig:cumulative_regret}),
    \textbf{No~forgetting} ($\gamma{=}1.0$) achieves the
    \emph{lowest} regret ($73.7$).
    \textbf{BanditGPT (adaptive~$\gamma$)} ranks second ($84.5$,
    $\Delta{=}{+}10.8$ vs.\ No~forgetting,
    95\%\,CI $[{+}8.9, {+}12.7]$, $p < 10^{-13}$),
    but \emph{significantly} outperforms fixed
    $\gamma{=}0.999$ ($95.6$, $\Delta{=}{-}11.1$,
    95\%\,CI $[-13.2, -9.0]$, $p < 10^{-12}$),
    Tabula~Rasa ($106.1$), and Fast~forgetting ($138.3$).
    %
    The reason No~forgetting wins: the cost change is an
    \emph{external signal} that the router observes immediately
    via the updated registry---no reward model adaptation is needed.
    The well-calibrated $\hat\theta$ estimates from Phase~1 remain
    valid (rewards did not change), and Gemini's UCB score jumps by
    $\lambda_s \times 0.67 = 0.134$ from the cost reduction alone.
    %
    The adaptive mechanism correctly identifies that prediction
    residuals have \emph{not} increased (the reward model is still
    accurate) and largely suppresses forgetting
    (Figure~\ref{fig:gamma_trajectory}), recovering 11.1 regret
    points vs.\ fixed $\gamma{=}0.999$ which blindly discounts
    still-valid $\hat\theta$ estimates.
    %
    \emph{Taken together with the reward swap experiment, these
    results demonstrate that adaptive forgetting correctly
    discriminates between shift types: it triggers when reward
    predictions deteriorate and stays quiescent when they remain
    valid---the key property motivating a residual-based detector
    over a fixed schedule.}
    }
    \label{fig:cost_shift_regret}
\end{figure*}
